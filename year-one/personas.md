# NR2F1 Foundation — Personas

> Who is most valuable to focus on **right now**?

## Our Mission

Our mission at NR2F1 Foundation is to empower families and individuals living
with rare NR2F1 variants through **education**, **advocacy** and **research**.

## Our Vision

Our vision is that every single family and individual living with rare NR2F1
variants will live a full and empowered life.

## 1. Newly diagnosed parents of patient or patient

> As a parent or patient who has just been diagnosed with BBSOAS
> I need to understand my diagnosis
> so that I can take the right steps towards managing the condition

**Who they are:** Usually parents with a child diagnosed with BBSOAS. Often
discover the website by searching via Google or being directly referred by
medical professionals that diagnosed their child.

### Their needs

- Easily findable and easily navigable information about the syndrome
- What now…? Guides about how to improve treatment and quality of life for their
  newly diagnosed children
- Connect to family support groups

### Their challenges

- Often their own neurologists don't know about the syndrome
- No other information for them to find online

### Foundation needs

1. Need them to register with the foundation registry and Natural History registry
2. Need them to join the BBSOAS community on the social media channels
3. Need them to register for CRID _(note: good long-term but less of a priority in
   moment of diagnosis)_

## 2. Parents of patient or patient

> As a parent or patient already known to the foundation
> I need to keep up to date with the latest news and research
> so that I can find treatment for my child

**Who they are:** Usually parents with a child diagnosed with BBSOAS. Often
active in Facebook family support group and/or at Foundation events.

### Their needs

- Keep up to date with latest research and news about the syndrome
- Stay connected with other families

### Their challenges

- Why check the website? Being able to see new information on website

### Foundation needs

1. Need them to easily keep up to date with the Foundation's news, events and activities
2. Need them to start fundraising with their networks

---

## 3. Foundation supporter

> As someone who wants to support people with BBSOAS
> I need to know how I can help
> so that I support the foundation fulfill its mission

**Who they are:** Often friends or family of diagnosed patients that donate
through small campaigns. Are often the ones to organise campaigns.

### Their needs

- Easily make donations of any amount
- Easily create and run campaigns with the right information and materials
- Know how to volunteer time and effort

### Their challenges

- Not sure how to set up a campaign with the Foundation

### Foundation needs

1. Need donations
2. Increase in monthly recurring donations, preferably recurring donations that we
   can count on
3. Need volunteers

_(note: HMW enable legacy gifts in the near future?)_

## 4. Medical professional with a diagnosed patient

> As a medical professional with a diagnosed patient
> I need to understand the condition
> so that I best manage the patient's care

### Their needs

- Can locate the website in the top 5 search results
- Can locate the guide for parents and families offered on our website
- Can find information tailored for the medical community

### Their challenges

- May lack credible sources of information for themselves and for their patients

### Foundation needs

1. Longer term goal: know a network of doctors that we can recommend to newly
   diagnosed families

_(future user story: medical network, data capture form?)_

## 5. Scientific researcher

> As a scientific researcher
> I need to understand what activity is underway
> so that I can become involved in NR2F1 research

### Their needs

- Locate summary data on our Natural History study
- Have access to information on disease models and bio-repository
- Has information on grants available

### Their challenges

- Can't find this information on the website

### Foundation needs

1. Giving them access to the information needed for their research: bio-repository
   etc. Infrastructure & inventory of current progress is already there to enable
   research. There are resources to already get involved.
2. Give information on grants
3. Showing people that you have a sizeable-enough population that will engagement
   and engage in trials
